Privacy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

This website

buildwithrex.me is a static website. It has no login, no forms, no analytics and no cookies set by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Instagram comments and DMs

When you comment a keyword (for example SCALE) on a @buildwith.rex reel, an automation service called ReplyKaro, connected through Instagram's official API, replies to your comment and sends you a DM with the free kit. To do that it processes your Instagram user ID, username and the comment. We use this only to send you what you asked for, and we do not sell or share it. We may send occasional DMs about new free kits; reply STOP and we will not message you again.

Your choices

To have your data removed, DM @buildwith.rex on Instagram with "delete my data". You can also remove the conversation from your own inbox at any time.
